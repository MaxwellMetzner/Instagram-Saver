Settings

General

How the extension looks and what clicking its icon does.

Theme

System follows your computer's light or dark setting.

Posts & stories

For a single post, reel, story, or highlight. Carousels and stories can hold several photos and videos.

Default download

Used for one-click downloads, and highlighted first in the popup. You can always pick the other option in the popup or right-click menu.

Profiles & saved posts

Quick download saves the newest posts in one go. Bulk download works through every post in the background and can be paused and resumed.

How many of the newest posts to include, from 1 to 200.

What to include

Applies to quick and bulk downloads.

Bulk download speed

Instagram limits how fast accounts can load posts. When it says "slow down", the bulk download pauses and tells you when it can continue.

Files & folders

Where downloads go inside your browser's Downloads folder, and what they're called.

Placeholders

Click one to add it to the field you were last editing.
